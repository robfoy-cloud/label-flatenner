How it works. The bottle's two outlines and the label's side edges meet at a vanishing point. Looking down the bottle, the outlines are tangents to a circle, which gives the true angle around the bottle for every vertical line. Rows are placed along each line with the cross-ratio, and each grid cell is mapped flat with its own homography. Naive spaces the columns evenly in the photo instead, which squashes the ends.

Errors are measured on the label in millimetres: every pixel of the recovered label is traced back through the true geometry to the point it really shows. The bottle is 74 mm across; the photo is 720 × 540 px with a phone-like lens.
